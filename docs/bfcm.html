Why this weekend matters

Poor tracking can cost you sales every day, but the impact is even greater during Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

Double-fired purchases

If the Purchase event fires twice, ad platforms see orders that never happened, and keep spending on what looks like a winner.

Missing server-side events

Ad blockers and browser privacy settings hide real sales from browser-only pixels. No server-side event means those orders go unseen.

Numbers that disagree

When GA4, Meta and your store all report a different order count, at least one of them is wrong, and you're making decisions on it.

What I check

Every store is evaluated using my 10-point checklist to ensure a complete review. The free scan checks everything we can see from the outside. Two of the points (05 and 06) require access to your advertising and analytics accounts, so they are included in the paid audit.

01
Pixel coveragePageView, ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, Purchase — missing or double-firing.
02
Purchase integrityValue, currency and order ID present; one fire per order.
03
Server-side eventsIs a server event (CAPI) paired with the browser event?
04
DeduplicationBrowser and server events share an event ID, so nothing counts twice.

Match qualityHow well ad platforms can match your conversions to real people. Needs account access.

09

10
